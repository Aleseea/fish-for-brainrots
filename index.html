Trade calculator

Is a trade fair? Put up to 6 brainrots on each side, exactly as they are (level, evolution, mutation and traits), to see what each side earns per second today, what each could earn once fully upgraded, and a verdict.

⚠️ Watch out for scams. Check the other player first (the /check command in the Fish for Brainrots Discord), record the trade, and make sure the items are the ones you agreed on. More safety tips on the wiki.
